Understanding Work Order Item Hierarchy
In construction and engineering projects, work order items typically follow a hierarchical structure:
Main Item (Level 1)
├── Sub Item (Level 2)
│   ├── Sub-Sub Item (Level 3)
│   │   ├── Sub-Sub-Sub Item (Level 4)
│   │   └── Sub-Sub-Sub Item (Level 4)
│   └── Sub-Sub Item (Level 3)
└── Sub Item (Level 2)
Importance Hierarchy
Main Items (1.0, 2.0, 3.0) - Major work categories
Sub Items (1.1, 1.2, 1.3) - Specific work types within categories
Sub-Sub Items (1.1.1, 1.1.2) - Detailed components
Sub-Sub-Sub Items (1.1.1.1, 1.1.1.2) - Granular tasks/materials
